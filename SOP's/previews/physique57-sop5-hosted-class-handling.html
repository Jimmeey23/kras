1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This SOP governs the handling of attendees at hosted classes, influencer events, brand collaboration classes, community workshops, and any other non-standard group experience at Physique 57 India. Hosted events are one of the highest-quality lead generation channels — attendees arrive with social proof already in place and a positive emotional experience primed. The conversion potential is high, but only if the post-event follow-up is structured and immediate.

All Sales & Client Servicing Executives involved in event planning or post-event follow-up

Team Leaders at event locations

Studio Operations team

Marketing team (for campaign coordination)

All hosted class formats: influencer classes, brand partner events, community workshops, corporate classes

4. LEAD CAPTURE AT HOSTED EVENTS

LEAD CAPTURE IS NON-NEGOTIABLE AT EVERY EVENT
Every attendee at every hosted event must leave a name and phone number — no exceptions.
A hosted class with no lead capture is a brand investment with zero ROI measurement and zero follow-up pipeline.
The lead capture must feel like a natural part of the event experience — not a transactional form-fill.
Digital RSVP forms are preferred over paper sign-in sheets — they eliminate manual data entry and reduce errors.

4.2 Event Lead CRM Entry Standards

All event leads must be in CRM within 24 hours of event end — no exceptions

Source tag: EVENT-[event type]-[name or brand] — e.g. EVENT-INFLUENCER-SHRADDHA, EVENT-CORPORATE-KPMG

Note the event name and date in lead notes field

Tag the format experienced at the event (Barre / PowerCycle / Strength Lab)

Note if lead is a known follower of the influencer or connected to the brand partner — affects follow-up tone

6. INFLUENCER & CREATOR SPECIFIC HANDLING

6.1 Why Influencer Events Need Separate Handling

Influencer-hosted class attendees are a distinct lead type. They came because of their trust in the host — not because they independently discovered Physique 57. This means the brand impression must be immaculate, the experience must be word-of-mouth worthy, and the follow-up must feel like a personal invitation — not a mass sales broadcast.

6.2 Influencer Lead Handling Protocol

All influencer class attendees are classified as HOT leads by default — they have overcome the awareness and curiosity barriers by attending

Post-event follow-up must be initiated within 4 hours of the event ending — not the next day

The first follow-up message must reference the specific event and influencer by name — generic messages will feel impersonal and reduce conversion

DO NOT send a mass broadcast to influencer class attendees — each follow-up message must feel individual and specific

If the influencer shared content about the class on social media — reference that content in the follow-up to create a personalised connection

Offer a specific next step: not 'let me know if you are interested' but 'I would love to book you in for a [free trial / Newcomers pack] this week — which day works for you?'

6.3 Handling the Influencer or Host Themselves

The influencer or host of the event is a VIP. Refer to SOP 7.0 (VIP Handling) for full protocol. Key points specific to influencers:

The influencer's personal experience must be managed by the Team Leader or Head of Sales — not a junior executive

Their content, their satisfaction, and their relationship with P57 must be proactively maintained beyond the event day

If the influencer is not already a member — a membership offer (approved by Head of Sales) should be extended personally and promptly

All influencer relationships are logged in CRM with the VIP tag and managed with a regular touchpoint cadence

7. POST-EVENT FOLLOW-UP & CONVERSION CADENCE

TOUCHPOINTTIMINGCHANNELMESSAGE APPROACH
E1 — Event Thank YouWithin 4 hours of event endWhatsAppPersonal thank-you. Reference the specific event and what was special about it. Warm and brief. No hard commercial ask.
E2 — Trial OfferDay 1 post-eventWhatsApp + Call for hot signalsOffer a specific trial or next class. Reference their interest or engagement from the event conversation.
E3 — Value Follow-UpDay 3 post-eventWhatsAppAdd value: share a class schedule, a relevant community update, or a success story relevant to their goals.
E4 — Conversion PushDay 7 post-eventWhatsApp + CallMembership recommendation. Forward-moving close question. Specific package recommendation based on discovery.
E5 — Final AttemptDay 14 post-eventWhatsAppRe-engagement. Re-offer a trial if not yet attended. Low pressure — leave the door wide open.
POST-EVENT FOLLOW-UP SPEED IS CRITICAL
Event leads NOT followed up within 24 hours lose 60–70% of their conversion potential.
Every hour of delay after an event reduces the prospect's emotional connection to the experience.
The team must know their post-event follow-up assignments BEFORE the event begins — not after.

9. REVENUE ATTRIBUTION FOR HOSTED EVENTS

All revenue generated from hosted event attendees must be attributed to the event source throughout the conversion journey. This enables accurate event ROI calculation and informs future event investment decisions.

The EVENT source tag must be maintained in CRM throughout the full sales cycle — do not reclassify to ORGANIC or other sources upon conversion

At point of conversion, the executive must confirm the original event source in the CRM record

Monthly reports must include: total event-attributed leads, trials booked, conversions, and revenue generated

Team Leader submits an event ROI summary to Head of Sales within 7 days of each hosted event
