1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This SOP defines the handling standards for Physique 57 India's VIP members and prospects. VIPs are individuals whose relationship with the studio — by virtue of influence, spend, profile, or relationship type — warrants a higher standard of service, greater discretion, and more senior engagement than the standard member journey. The goal is to ensure every VIP feels genuinely valued, personally known, and effortlessly served at all times.

All Sales & Client Servicing Executives who may encounter or be assigned VIP accounts

All Team Leaders responsible for VIP oversight

Head of Sales & Client Servicing — owns all VIP relationships at the senior level

Studio Operations team — must be briefed on VIP arrivals and preferences

2. VIP CLASSIFICATION AT PHYSIQUE 57

Physique 57 India recognises four categories of VIP members. Each category has distinct handling requirements. All categories receive elevated service — the differences lie in discretion levels, engagement ownership, and specific protocols.

THE VIP PHILOSOPHY
VIP status is not about revenue alone — it is about the level of trust, discretion, and personal service the relationship requires.
A VIP who feels like just another member will not renew. A VIP who feels known, valued, and effortlessly served will stay for years and refer others.
Every member of the team who interacts with a VIP is representing the studio's ability to hold that relationship with care.

4.2 VIP Onboarding Experience

When a VIP joins as a new member, their onboarding experience must be elevated beyond the standard member onboarding. The following are minimum requirements:

Personal welcome message from the Team Leader or Head of Sales — not from an executive

Dedicated orientation session if requested: studio tour, format introduction, trainer meeting

Preferred class times and instructor preferences noted in CRM before first class

Front desk team briefed on the VIP's preferences, privacy requirements, and name before their first visit

For Category A: confirm whether they wish to be acknowledged publicly in class or prefer discretion

5. ANNUAL & HIGH-VALUE MEMBER VIP STANDARDS

High-Value Annual Members (Category B) represent the studio's most committed community. Their loyalty deserves active, proactive recognition — not just transactional service.

5.1 Ongoing Relationship Standards

Dedicated executive assigned to each VIP-HIGHVALUE account. That executive owns the relationship.

Minimum monthly proactive touchpoint — not renewal-related. A check-in, a milestone acknowledgement, a community update.

Attendance milestones must be tracked and celebrated: 50 classes, 100 classes, 200 classes, annual anniversary

Attendance patterns must be monitored — any drop-off triggers an immediate personal check-in (not a template)

VIP-HIGHVALUE members must never be allowed to lapse silently — Team Leader must be aware of any absence beyond 7 days

6. CELEBRITY & PUBLIC FIGURE HANDLING

6.1 Discretion as Default

Category A VIPs attend Physique 57 for their own personal wellness — not for brand association, press exposure, or social media content. All handling must treat discretion as the default setting, not an exception.

CELEBRITY VIP PRIVACY — ABSOLUTE RULES
NEVER share the identity, attendance schedule, or contact details of a Category A VIP with anyone — including other members, other staff, or third parties — without explicit written consent from the VIP.
NEVER photograph, film, or share content featuring a Category A VIP without their explicit prior consent.
NEVER reference a Category A VIP's attendance or membership in any marketing or social media content without written consent.
Any breach of VIP privacy is a serious disciplinary matter regardless of intention.

6.2 Studio Floor Protocol for Category A VIPs

Front desk team is briefed on Category A VIP arrivals before the class — name recognition without public acknowledgement

VIP is greeted warmly and privately — not announced or greeted with excess fanfare in front of other members

If other members approach or recognise the VIP: staff must remain neutral and not facilitate or encourage the interaction

If the VIP requests a private locker, specific class spot, or any other accommodation: fulfil it immediately without discussion

Post-class: do not linger. Allow them to leave naturally without extended staff engagement unless they initiate

7. CORPORATE CLIENT VIP HANDLING

Corporate clients require a professional, solution-oriented relationship management approach that differs from individual member handling. The key stakeholders are often HR or wellness leads at the corporate — the individual attendees are secondary beneficiaries.

7.1 Corporate Account Structure

One primary corporate contact is identified and owned by the Head of Sales

All commercial discussions (pricing, event planning, renewals, escalations) go through the Head of Sales

Day-to-day member management for corporate employees is handled by the assigned executive

Corporate accounts must have a quarterly review call with the Head of Sales — health of relationship, usage, upcoming needs

7.2 Corporate Event & Class Handling

When a corporate class is booked (see SOP 5.0 for full event handling), the following additional standards apply:

Confirm headcount and employee list with the corporate contact at least 5 days before the class

Ensure all employees are treated with the same standard of welcome as individual members — they are potential future individual members

Post-event: send the corporate contact a brief summary of attendance and offer to plan the next session

Individual employees who express personal interest must be followed up with as standard leads within 24 hours

8. PRIVATE CLASS CLIENT HANDLING

Private class clients (Category D VIPs) have a fundamentally different relationship with P57 — it is built on personal transformation, trust in a specific trainer, and a high level of financial investment. The handling must reflect the intimacy and intentionality of that relationship.

8.1 Private Client Standards

Every private client has a named executive and a named trainer — both are accountable for the relationship

The executive must know the client's goals, progress indicators, schedule preferences, and any personal context shared

Any change to the client's trainer must be communicated by the executive personally before it happens — never discovered by the client on arrival

Pack renewal conversations happen at 3 sessions remaining — not when the pack expires

Private clients must be checked in personally on every visit — not left to self-check-in

8.2 Trainer-Executive Coordination for Private Clients

Weekly check-in between assigned executive and assigned trainer on each private client — even just 2 minutes

If a trainer flags any concern about a private client (health, emotional state, progress stall) — the executive must follow up that week

If a private client wishes to change trainers: escalate to Team Leader immediately. Handle with extreme care.

12. VIP CRM STANDARDS

VIP tag (VIP-CELEBRITY / VIP-HIGHVALUE / VIP-CORPORATE / VIP-PRIVATE) applied immediately upon identification

All VIP records require more detailed notes than standard members — include preferences, sensitivities, personal context shared, goals, and milestone history

VIP records are visible only to: assigned executive, Team Leader, Head of Sales, and COO. Junior staff without account ownership must not access VIP full profiles.

Communication logs must be complete — every call, message, and in-person interaction noted with date, content summary, and outcome

VIP accounts are reviewed by the Team Leader weekly as part of the standard account health review

13. CONFIDENTIALITY & PRIVACY RULES

CONFIDENTIALITY — ABSOLUTE & NON-NEGOTIABLE
All VIP member information is strictly confidential — name, schedule, contact details, personal context, and membership details.
VIP information may not be shared with other members, external parties, or non-essential staff under any circumstances.
Social media posts, stories, or any content that could identify a VIP's presence at P57 are prohibited without explicit written consent.
Any team member who shares VIP information without authorisation will face immediate disciplinary action.
If a VIP consents to be featured in marketing — this consent must be documented in writing in CRM before any content is shared.
