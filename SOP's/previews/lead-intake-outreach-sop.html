1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

1.1 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs the complete lead intake and management lifecycle for the Physique 57 India Sales and Client Servicing Department. Its purpose is to ensure every prospective client who enquires about Physique 57 — regardless of source, location, or format — receives a consistent, high-quality, and timely response that maximises conversion potential and upholds the brand's premium positioning.

This SOP eliminates dependency on individual memory, informal habits, and reactive execution by replacing them with a structured, auditable, and scalable lead management system.

1.2 Scope

This SOP applies to:

All Sales & Client Servicing Executives across all Physique 57 India locations

All Team Leaders managing front-line sales teams

The Head of Sales & Client Servicing

All leads generated through any channel — digital, walk-in, referral, influencer, or direct

Both Mumbai locations: Kwality House & Supreme HQ

Bengaluru location: Kenkere House

Copper N Cloves pop-up location

1.3 Objectives

This SOP is designed to achieve the following operational outcomes:

Zero lead leakage — every enquiry is captured, owned, and followed up

First response within defined SLA windows across all lead sources

Consistent, brand-appropriate communication at every touchpoint

Structured follow-up that does not rely on human memory

CRM hygiene that enables accurate reporting and management visibility

Differentiated lead handling by source, format, location, and trial eligibility

Accountable follow-up compliance trackable by team leaders and management

Improved trial-to-sale and lead-to-trial conversion rates

8.4 Follow-Up Communication Standards

All follow-up communication must adhere to the following standards regardless of channel:

Every message must feel personal and warm — not templated or robotic. Use the client's first name.

WhatsApp messages must be concise — no more than 4–5 lines. Long messages are rarely read.

Voice notes are encouraged for Day 3+ follow-ups — they are harder to ignore and more personal.

Do not send promotional messages or pricing unprompted before Day 3 — lead trust must be built first.

Never send the same message twice. Each touchpoint must add new value or a new angle.

Never contact a lead more than once on the same day unless they initiate further conversation.

Do not follow up past 8:00 PM or before 8:00 AM under any circumstances.

9.2 Trial Rebooking Protocol

If a lead cancels or needs to reschedule their trial, the following protocol applies:

Acknowledge the cancellation graciously — never express disappointment or pressure

Immediately offer 2–3 new available slots for rescheduling

Reschedule within the same communication — do not leave the rebooking open-ended

Update CRM with new booking date. Mark previous date as 'Rescheduled', not 'Cancelled'.

Maximum 2 rescheduling attempts per lead. After the 2nd reschedule with no attendance, move to COLD tier.

9.3 No-Show Protocol

When a lead fails to attend a confirmed trial without prior notice:

Mark attendance as NO-SHOW in CRM within 1 hour of class end

Send a warm, non-accusatory check-in message within 2 hours (see template in Section 13)

Attempt a call the following morning if no response to WhatsApp

If no response within 48 hours of no-show: reoffer trial booking

If no response within 7 days of no-show: move to COLD tier and standard cadence

After 2 no-shows: escalate to Team Leader before any further outreach

10.2 Common Objections & Recommended Responses

OBJECTIONRECOMMENDED RESPONSE APPROACH
'It's too expensive'Acknowledge. Reframe around per-session value and long-term results. Compare to alternatives only if it helps. Offer a shorter commitment package to lower entry point.
'I need to think about it'Ask what specifically they need to think about — this often uncovers the real objection. Offer to address it directly.
'I need to check my schedule'Help them — pull up the class schedule immediately. Offer to book a tentative slot that they can change.
'I'll start next month'Ask what changes next month. Acknowledge their reasoning. Suggest starting with a 2-week pack to 'test the commitment' now.
'I want to try a few more classes'Ask which format or instructor they want to try. Offer a 4-class or 8-class pack to continue exploring without commitment.
'I'm comparing with other studios'Acknowledge and respect. Ask what's most important to them in a studio. Reposition Physique 57's specific differentiators for that priority.
'Can I get a discount?'Never discount without approval. Acknowledge the ask warmly. Offer to check with the team leader. Do not promise a discount.
DISCOUNT AUTHORITY — NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE
Executives are NEVER authorised to offer discounts unilaterally.
Any discount or pricing exception MUST be approved by the Team Leader before being communicated to the lead.
Communicating an unapproved discount — even verbally — is a serious compliance breach.
If a lead pushes for a discount, say: 'Let me check what we can do and get back to you in a few minutes.'

12. LEAD RE-ENGAGEMENT & LONG-TERM NURTURE

12.1 Re-engagement Triggers

The following conditions trigger a re-engagement attempt:

Lead has been unresponsive for 15+ days after active follow-up

Lead was previously marked COLD and is now being revisited after 30-day cooling period

A seasonal campaign, new class format, or special offer has launched

A lead's previously stated 'start date' has now arrived (e.g. 'I'll start after Diwali')

A new location or format relevant to the lead has launched

12.2 Re-engagement Message Principles

Never reference how long it has been — it signals the executive lost track

Always lead with something new: a new offer, a new class, a new time slot, a new event

Keep it short — 2–3 lines maximum. A long re-engagement message will not be read.

Voice notes perform significantly better than text for re-engagement after a long gap

One re-engagement attempt per 30-day window. Do not over-contact dormant leads.

12.3 Archive Protocol

A lead is eligible for archiving when ALL of the following conditions are met:

Minimum 8 contact attempts have been made across the full follow-up cadence

The lead has been in the CRM for at least 60 days with no meaningful response

A final re-engagement message has been sent and received no response within 7 days

Team Leader has reviewed and approved the archiving decision

When archiving, the executive must tag the reason for archiving (no response / moved / not interested / budget / competitor) in CRM. Archived leads are retained in the CRM and are eligible for campaign-based outreach in future.

14.2 CRM Notes Standards

Notes in CRM must be written to a standard that allows any team member — including a new executive, a Team Leader, or the Head of Sales — to understand the full history of a lead without asking the primary executive.

Every note must include: date, contact method, outcome, and next step

Notes must be written in English — third-person format (e.g. 'Lead confirmed interest in Barre. Trial booked for 14 May.')

No abbreviations that would be unclear to another team member

No personal opinions about the lead's personality or behaviour in notes

Outcome of every call must be noted — even if it was 'no answer'

17. COMMON SCENARIOS & DECISION GUIDE

Scenario A — Lead enquires about Barre at Bengaluru

Offer: First class at 50% off (not free). If they ask about a free trial: explain that our Bengaluru introductory offer is the discounted first class, followed by the Newcomers 2-for-1 pack. Do not offer a Mumbai policy at a Bengaluru location.

Scenario B — Lead enquires about Strength Lab and is in Bengaluru

Strength Lab is only available at Kwality House, Mumbai. Acknowledge their interest. Ask if they visit Mumbai. If yes — offer a Mumbai trial. If no — offer them a Barre or PowerCycle trial in Bengaluru. Do not create false expectations.

Scenario C — Lead is a referral from an existing client

Check CRM history: find the referring client's record and note the referral. Priority-book a trial within 24 hours of first contact. Acknowledge the referring client's name warmly. Inform the referring client that the referral has been received (if your studio has a referral programme).

Scenario D — Lead attended a trial but is now unresponsive

Follow the post-trial follow-up cadence in full (Section 8.3) before moving to cold status. A minimum of 5 post-trial attempts must be made. Do not assume disinterest after 1–2 unanswered messages. Use a voice note for attempt 4 or 5.

Scenario E — Lead asks for a price they heard was different

Do not honour unapproved prices. Say: 'Let me confirm the current pricing for you and come back to you shortly.' Check with Team Leader if there is any active offer or exception in place. Never confirm a price you are uncertain about.

Scenario F — Lead at Copper N Cloves pop-up asks about trials

No trial offers are available at pop-up locations. Offer a single class at standard pricing. If the lead is interested in a longer commitment, direct them to the nearest Mumbai studio and arrange a follow-up booking.

Scenario G — New lead matches an existing client in CRM

Do not create a new lead record. Check if the person is a former client. If yes — use the Reactivation flow and tag as REACTIVATION. Update the existing record. Inform Team Leader before making contact.
