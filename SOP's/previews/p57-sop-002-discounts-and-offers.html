1. PURPOSE & REVENUE PHILOSOPHY

This SOP governs how discounts, offers, and promotional incentives are structured, approved, and deployed at Physique 57. Discounts are a revenue acceleration tool — not a default sales tactic. When applied strategically, they convert hesitant leads, reward loyalty, and reactivate lapsed clients. When applied arbitrarily, they erode brand value, create pricing inconsistency, and undermine team accountability.

This SOP defines:

What discounts exist and who can offer them without approval

What discounts require approval and from whom

How offer campaigns should be structured to drive revenue

How discount compliance is monitored and audited

RULE #1No executive may offer a discount, extend an offer, or create a discount code that is not listed in this document without prior written approval from Jimmeey (Head — Sales & Client Servicing). Verbal approvals do not count.
RULE #2Discounting to close a sale is the LAST resort, not the first. A discount offered before objection-handling has failed is a lost margin opportunity.

2. STANDING DISCOUNT STRUCTURE

The following discounts are pre-approved and may be offered by any executive without seeking additional approval. These are the only discounts that can be communicated to clients without escalation.

30-Class PackStudio 30 Single Class Pack10%Any executiveStanding — no expiry. Position as best per-class value for irregular schedules.

Annual MembershipAll formats — annual unlimited15%Any executiveStanding. Always present annual as the highest-value, lowest cost-per-class option.

Applying Standing Discounts — Important Rules

Standing discounts should be presented as part of the value, not as a concession: 'The 6-month membership already includes a 10% reduction — so you're getting 180 days at a significantly better rate.'

Never layer standing discounts on top of each other unless explicitly approved in writing

The Newcomers 15% applies ONLY to the first purchase. If the client returns after a lapse, it does not re-apply automatically.

If a client asks 'can I get a discount?' and a standing discount already applies to their package — communicate the standing discount clearly before considering any additional reduction

4. DISCOUNT CODE CREATION PROTOCOL

Discount codes may only be created by Jimmeey or the designated person authorised by Jimmeey. No executive should be creating discount codes independently in the booking/membership system.

How to Request a Discount Code

Step 1 — Confirm discount approval via the authority matrix above

Step 2 — Request code creation by messaging Jimmeey with: client name, package, approved discount %, expiry date required

Step 3 — Code is created by Jimmeey or designated admin and communicated back within the defined SLA

Step 4 — Executive applies code and confirms purchase completion to Jimmeey

RULEDiscount codes are single-use and client-specific unless explicitly stated otherwise. Generic, reusable codes must be approved by Jimmeey for every campaign they are used in.

5. REVENUE-BOOSTING OFFER CAMPAIGNS

Beyond standing discounts, the following structured campaigns are recommended for driving revenue at specific points in the sales and retention lifecycle. Each campaign requires Jimmeey's approval before launch.

Campaign 1 — Post-Trial Conversion Offer

Objective: Convert trial clients who are hesitating post-trial

TriggerLead has attended trial but has not purchased within 5–7 days
OfferNewcomers 15% on first purchase (already standing) + added urgency: 'This offer is valid for 7 days from your trial date'
ChannelWhatsApp (personal message, not broadcast) + one phone call
Message Script'Hi [Name], it was great having you in class! As a new member, your 15% welcome discount is valid until [date]. Would love to help you get started — shall I hold a spot for you?'
Who Runs ItExecutive handling the lead

Campaign 2 — Lapsed Client Reactivation

Objective: Bring back clients who have been inactive for 30–90 days

TriggerClient membership expired or client has not attended in 30–90 days
OfferUp to 15% on renewal — requires Jimmeey approval
ChannelPersonal WhatsApp message first, followed by a phone call if no response in 48 hours
Message Script'Hi [Name], we have been missing you at the studio! We have a special returning member offer this month — would love to get you back. Can I share the details?'
FrequencyRun monthly — Jimmeey identifies lapsed client list from CRM/membership system

Campaign 3 — Early Renewal Incentive

Objective: Drive renewals before membership expiry — proactive, not reactive

TriggerMembership expiry is 14 days away
OfferUpgrade offer: 'Renew for 3 months or more in the next 14 days and we will add 7 complimentary days to your membership.' (Requires Jimmeey approval)
ChannelWhatsApp personal message — do not use broadcast for this. Then phone call.
Message Script'Hi [Name], your membership is coming up for renewal on [date]. Renewing this month for 3 months or more gets you an extra week on us. Want me to sort this out for you?'
Who Runs ItExecutive owning the client relationship

Campaign 4 — Referral Reward Programme

Objective: Leverage highest-converting lead source — referred clients

Referrer RewardActive member who refers a friend who purchases a membership receives: 1 complimentary class credit per successful referral (up to 3 per month)
Referred Lead OfferNewcomers 15% (already standing) — communicate explicitly: 'Your friend [Name] has referred you — your welcome discount is already applied.'
TrackingExecutive must log referring client name in CRM at point of lead entry. Referral credit issued only upon confirmed purchase.
ApprovalReferral credit issuance requires Jimmeey acknowledgement

Campaign 5 — Influencer / Hosted Class Conversion

Objective: Convert hosted class attendees — warm, high-trust leads

TriggerAttendee participated in a hosted class but did not purchase within 3 days
OfferNewcomers 15% (standing) communicated with a personal, warm message tied to the host's name
Message Script'Hi [Name], it was lovely having you at [Host Name]'s class! If you would like to continue your journey with us, your first membership includes [Host]'s welcome discount. Happy to help you get set up.'
TimingFirst outreach within 24 hours of hosted class. Maximum 3 follow-up attempts over 7 days.

6. WHAT NEVER TO DO — DISCOUNT MISUSE

These are the most common discount misuse patterns observed in boutique fitness sales. All are prohibited at Physique 57.

Prohibited BehaviourWhy It Is Harmful
Offering a discount before any objection is raisedTrains clients to always wait for discounts. Erodes brand positioning and reduces your ability to close at full price.
Telling a client 'let me see what I can do' without authorityCreates false expectations, delays conversion, and often results in a discount that was never needed.
Offering a discount verbally, then creating the code laterCreates untracked discounts, inconsistent records, and no audit trail.
Applying the same discount to every client 'because it is easier'Eliminates differentiation, reduces urgency for full-price sales, and distorts revenue reporting.
Stacking the Newcomers 15% on top of another approved discountResults in margins below acceptable thresholds. Never combine unless explicitly approved.
Creating discount codes without authorisationDirect policy violation. Results in untracked discounts and disciplinary action.

7. DISCOUNT REPORTING & GOVERNANCE

All discounts applied must be tracked and reported. This is non-negotiable. Without visibility into discounting patterns, revenue leakage cannot be identified or corrected.

Weekly Discount Log

Every executive must log all discounts offered during the week in their daily report

Log format: Client name | Package | Standard price | Discount applied | Approval reference | Date

Jimmeey reviews weekly discount log every Monday

Monthly Discount Audit

Accounts team (Sachin) cross-references discounts applied in membership system against approval records

Any unmatched discount is flagged immediately

Recurring unauthorised discounting by any individual is addressed in performance review
