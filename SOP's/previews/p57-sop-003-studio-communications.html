1. PURPOSE & COMMUNICATION PHILOSOPHY

Every message sent or received by a Physique 57 team member — whether on WhatsApp, Yellow Messenger, email, or a phone call — is a representation of the brand. Communication is not just a logistical function. It is a client experience touchpoint.

This SOP defines how every communication channel is used, what response standards apply, what content standards must be maintained, and what is strictly prohibited. It applies to every executive, team leader, and manager who interacts with leads, active clients, or internal teams.

PRINCIPLERespond fast. Respond right. Every interaction either builds trust or erodes it. There is no neutral communication.

3. WHATSAPP BUSINESS — BEST PRACTICES

WhatsApp is the primary communication channel for client interaction at Physique 57. Used correctly, it is the most effective tool in your sales and servicing arsenal. Used incorrectly, it destroys lead conversions, damages client relationships, and creates unmanageable workloads.

3.1 Device & Account Rules

Every studio has one dedicated WhatsApp Business number. This is the ONLY number to be used for all client communication.

Personal WhatsApp numbers must NEVER be used for client or lead communication — regardless of convenience.

The studio device is shared between team members on shift. Executives do not communicate with clients from personal devices.

At no time should a client's contact be saved to a personal device for follow-up purposes.

If a client messages a personal number, politely redirect: 'Please use our studio number [number] so our full team can support you!'

3.2 Response Time Standards

Existing client queryWithin 1 hourAcknowledge within 1 hour. Resolution within 24 hours for complex issues.
Complaint or urgent issueWithin 30 minutesAcknowledge immediately. Escalate if needed per SOP-001.
Renewal reminder outreach (outbound)Send 14 days before expiryAlways personal — never a broadcast for renewal.
Post-trial follow-up (outbound)Within 4 hours of trialWhile energy is still fresh. Do not wait until next day.

3.3 Tone & Language Standards

Always greet by name: 'Hi [Name]!' — never 'Hi there', 'Hey', or no greeting

Warm but professional. Physique 57 is a premium boutique fitness brand — the language must reflect that

Use correct grammar and spelling. Typos and abbreviations like 'u', 'r', 'thr' are not acceptable in client communication

Emojis are permitted but should be used sparingly — maximum 1–2 per message, only where they add warmth

Voice notes: acceptable for brief, warm messages or check-ins — never for complex information, pricing, or sensitive topics

Never use voice notes for complaints, refund discussions, or any topic that requires a written record

3.5 What to NEVER Send on WhatsApp

Pricing without context or a conversation — do not blast package prices to cold leads

Lengthy paragraphs — break information into short messages. Maximum 3–4 lines per message bubble.

Sensitive information: refund decisions, account balances, incident details, staff discussions

Complaints from other clients — never forward or share another client's feedback

Promotional content to clients who have explicitly asked not to be contacted

Payment links or bank details without a conversation leading to them

4. YELLOW MESSENGER — PROTOCOLS

Yellow Messenger is used for broadcast messages, automated campaigns, and managing incoming query volumes at scale. It complements WhatsApp Business — it does not replace the personal outreach that converts leads and retains clients.

4.1 Approved Uses of Yellow Messenger

Use CaseHow to Use It
Broadcast — promotional campaignsUsed for offers, events, hosted classes, and membership campaigns. Must be approved by Jimmeey before sending. Segmentation required — do not send all broadcasts to all clients.
Automated welcome messageAuto-response to new inbound leads outside business hours: 'Hi! Thank you for reaching out to Physique 57. Our team will get back to you shortly during business hours.'
Trial remindersAutomated reminder 24 hours and 2 hours before a trial class. Must include: class name, time, location, and what to bring.
Renewal remindersAutomated reminder when membership is 14 days from expiry. Trigger for personal follow-up call/message from executive.
Incoming query routingYellow Messenger receives incoming queries during class hours and queues them for response when executive is free. Responses must still feel personal — not templated.
Post-class feedback (optional)Automated 'How was your class?' message sent 1 hour after class. Used selectively for new clients.

4.2 Broadcast Message Rules

All broadcast messages must be approved by Jimmeey before sending

Broadcasts must be segmented — active members, lapsed members, trial attendees, and leads are different audiences and require different messages

Do not send more than 2 broadcast messages per week to any single audience segment

Every broadcast must have a clear call to action: 'Reply to book', 'Click to purchase', 'Reply YES for more info'

Unsubscribe requests must be honoured immediately — remove from all broadcast lists within 24 hours

4.3 Template Approval

All WhatsApp templates used in Yellow Messenger must be pre-approved at the platform level

New templates require Jimmeey's review before submission for approval

Templates must not include pricing unless the pricing has been confirmed as current and accurate

Templates are reviewed and updated quarterly or whenever pricing/offers change

5. EMAIL COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS

Email is the formal record-keeping channel for Physique 57. All incidents, escalations, management decisions, and operational communications must be documented via email. Email is the only communication mode that creates an accountable, auditable trail.

5.1 Studio Email IDs

5.2 Email Etiquette Standards

Always use a professional email signature including: Name, Title, Studio, Phone, Email, Website

Subject lines must be specific and action-oriented — not generic ('Hi' or 'Update' are unacceptable subject lines)

Reply to emails within 24 hours — even if just to acknowledge and confirm next steps

Do not use informal language, slang, or abbreviations in any client-facing email

Always CC the right people — refer to the CC matrix in SOP-001 for incident emails

Do not CC people unnecessarily — large CC lists dilute accountability

5.3 Email Thread Discipline

The lack of email thread discipline has been identified as a recurring operational issue. The following rules are non-negotiable:

THREAD RULEAlways reply to the existing thread. Do not start a new email thread for an ongoing conversation or for adding a new incident to an existing monthly report. Use Reply All when all CCs need to remain informed.

Incident Reports: One thread per studio per month (see SOP-001 for full detail)

Feedback Reports: Separate thread per studio per month — not merged with Incident Report thread

Hosted Class Reports: Continue the ongoing Hosted Class thread per location — do not start a new thread per host

Operational requests (locker codes, facility issues, schedule changes): Start a new thread specific to the request. Use a clear subject line.

Management decisions: Confirmed via reply on the relevant thread — do not communicate decisions separately from the thread where the issue was raised

6. PHONE CALL STANDARDS

Phone calls are the most powerful first-touch tool for lead conversion and escalation management. A confident, warm, well-structured call outperforms any WhatsApp message. However, calls are underutilised because they feel more uncomfortable — which means the studios that make calls consistently have a significant competitive advantage.

6.1 When to Call

SituationCall Protocol
New lead (first contact)CALL FIRSTthen follow up on WhatsApp
Post-trial follow-up (Day 1)CALL FIRST— call within 4–24 hours of trial
Lead gone cold after 3+ daysCALL— a call breaks the pattern of ignored messages
P1 / P2 incident escalationMANDATORY CALL— before email. See SOP-001.
Renewal reminder (14 days out)CALL— personal renewal conversations have significantly higher conversion than messages
Retention interventionCALL— client absent 14+ days — warm check-in call

6.2 Call Structure — Lead First Contact

Opening: 'Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] calling from Physique 57 [Studio]. You enquired about us recently — did I catch you at a good time?'

Discovery: Ask 2–3 questions: 'What got you interested in Physique 57?', 'Have you tried barre / cycling / strength training before?', 'What are you hoping to get out of it?'

Value bridge: Connect their answer to the format — 'That is exactly what our [format] is designed for.'

Trial booking: 'The best way to experience it is to come in for a class — we offer a complimentary trial for barre. Can I book a spot for you this week?'

Close or follow-up: Confirm booking or set a specific follow-up: 'I will send you a WhatsApp with the details. Look out for it.'

6.3 Call Logging Rules

Every call with a lead or client must be logged in the CRM with: date, time, duration, outcome, and next action

If a call is unanswered, log the attempt and send a WhatsApp message immediately: 'Hi [Name], I just tried calling — this is [Name] from Physique 57. Happy to connect when you are free!'

Call attempts count toward follow-up sequences — three unanswered calls without a response shifts the lead to a lower priority bucket
