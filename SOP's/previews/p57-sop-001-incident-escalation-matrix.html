1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This SOP governs how incidents, complaints, and feedback are captured, categorised, escalated, and resolved across all Physique 57 studios. Every incident that occurs on or related to our premises must be formally reported, owned, resolved, and reviewed — without exception.

The objective is to eliminate ad-hoc, memory-dependent, or inconsistently communicated incident handling. Every issue that affects a client, a staff member, or the studio must move through a structured process — regardless of how small it may seem.

RULEIf it is not reported in writing within the SLA, it did not happen officially. Verbal communication alone does not constitute incident reporting.

6. SPECIFIC INCIDENT HANDLING PLAYBOOKS

INC-01 — Physical Health / Medical Event

This is the highest-risk incident category. Safety always takes priority over any studio policy.

Immediate Steps (On-Site)

Do not leave the client unattended under any circumstances

Ask the client calmly: 'Are you feeling okay? Can I get you some water?'

Move the client to a quiet, cool area away from the studio floor if they can be safely moved

If the client loses consciousness or is in acute distress — call emergency services (112) immediately

Do not attempt to diagnose or provide medical advice

Alert the trainer and team leader immediately — face to face or phone call

Reporting (Within 15 Minutes)

Call Jimmeey immediately — do not wait for the email

Send P1 incident report email within 15 minutes

Document everything: timeline, what was said, actions taken, outcome

Note whether CCTV footage exists and request it be preserved

Post-Incident

Follow up with client the next day — warm, empathetic check-in

Do not offer refunds, credits, or any form of compensation without Jimmeey's approval

Incident is reviewed in weekly team review

INC-02 — Missing Valuables / Theft Allegation

Treat all allegations seriously. Never dismiss, minimise, or argue with the client. This has significant legal and reputational implications.

Immediate Steps

Listen calmly. Do not be defensive. Do not imply the client is mistaken.

Say: 'I understand this is very upsetting. Let me involve my manager right away so we can look into this properly.'

Call Jimmeey and Mitali IMMEDIATELY — this is P1

Do not access or review CCTV yourself — this must be done with management present or authorised

Preserve the scene if relevant (locker area, studio floor)

Take written notes of exactly what the client reports (what was missing, when last seen, where stored)

Management Actions

CCTV review — with Zahur / facility team

Written statement from client requested (for records)

No admission of liability at any point

Legal and insurance implications reviewed by Mitali

INC-03 — Client Behavioral Misconduct

Disruptive or aggressive client behaviour toward staff, trainers, or other members must be documented and escalated. Physique 57 maintains a zero-tolerance environment for harassment.

During the Class (Trainer Role)

Trainer addresses the behaviour calmly and professionally in the moment

Trainer does not engage in an argument or escalate the situation

Trainer informs the executive on duty immediately after class

Post-Class (Executive Role)

Do not confront the client on the studio floor

Take a full statement from the trainer — written if possible

Document witnesses

Report to Jimmeey within 30 minutes with incident report email

Repeat Offenders

Second documented incident triggers a formal written warning from management

Third incident — management decision on membership suspension

No membership shall be cancelled without Jimmeey + Mitali approval

INC-04 — Competitor Activity on Premises

Any client found promoting, soliciting for, or actively representing a competitor brand inside a Physique 57 studio must be addressed.

Executive politely and privately asks the client to refrain from commercial activity inside the studio

Do not cause a public scene — handle discreetly

Report to Jimmeey and loop in Saachi (Operations) and Reyna (Marketing) per Saachi's directive

Document what was said, to whom, and the context (hosted class, regular class, etc.)

INC-05 — Trial Experience Failure

When a trial client leaves mid-session, seems upset, or reports a negative experience, this requires immediate and warm intervention.

Executive approaches the client personally — not via WhatsApp alone

Acknowledge their experience: 'I noticed you stepped out — I hope you are okay. Can I check in with you for a moment?'

Understand the root cause before offering any remedy

Do not offer a free class or discount without Team Leader approval

Report must be sent to Jimmeey same day (P2)

Follow up with a personal WhatsApp message within 4 hours of their departure

INC-06 — Trainer-Related Complaint

Trainer complaints are sensitive. Handle with care — do not dismiss client, do not side publicly with trainer, do not share details with other team members unnecessarily.

Executive listens without reacting — acknowledges client's experience: 'Thank you for sharing this with us.'

Escalate to Jimmeey within 2 hours

Jimmeey reviews and involves Anisha (Master Trainer) and/or respective Head Trainer

Client is never directly informed of any internal action taken — unless legally relevant

Resolution follows trainer management protocol under HR

INC-07 — Facility / Equipment Failure

Immediate Steps

Acknowledge the issue to the affected client

Raise to Zahur (Studio Operations) for facilities matters — include Saachi on email

Set a resolution SLA with Zahur and update the client

Example: 'We have raised this with our operations team and it will be resolved by [date/time]'

Reporting

Log in the incident report email within 24 hours

Follow up that the resolution was completed

7. EMAIL THREAD PROTOCOL FOR INCIDENT REPORTING

The existing practice of creating separate email threads for 'Incident Reports' and 'Feedback Reports' at some studios, and separate reports from different executives for the same studio, creates confusion and breaks auditability.

Standardised Thread Structure

Thread TypeFormat & Rules
Incident Report ThreadOne thread per studio per calendar month. Subject: 'Incident Report — [Studio Name] — [Month Year]'. All incidents for that studio during the month are added as REPLIES to the same thread. Do not create new threads for each incident.
Feedback Report ThreadOne separate thread per studio per calendar month. Subject: 'Feedback Report — [Studio Name] — [Month Year]'. Client service feedback, schedule comments, and suggestions go here.
Critical / P1 IncidentsP1 incidents may be sent as a separate email immediately for urgency, then added as a reply to the master monthly thread. Subject: '[URGENT — P1] [Incident Type] — [Studio] — [Date]'

Standard CC Structure

Recipient FieldWho
TO:Jimmeey (Head — Sales & Client Servicing)
CC (Always):Mitali (COO), Saachi (Head — Operations & Facilities), Zahur (Studio Operations)
CC (If relevant):Anisha / respective Head Trainer (for trainer-related incidents); Reyna (for hosted class or competitor-related incidents)
CRITICALDo NOT send incident reports to all studio staff in the TO field. Reports go to leadership and relevant ops only. Wider team communication, where needed, is handled by Jimmeey or Team Leader separately.

8. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW PROCESS

Every P1 and P2 incident is reviewed in the weekly team meeting. Every P3 and P4 incident is logged and reviewed monthly. The purpose is not blame — it is to identify process improvements, training gaps, and early warning patterns.

Weekly Review (P1 & P2)

Jimmeey reviews all P1 and P2 incidents from the prior week in the Monday team meeting

Was the incident reported on time?

Was the escalation path followed correctly?

Was the client resolution satisfactory?

What can be changed to prevent recurrence?

Monthly Review (All Incidents)

Saachi and Jimmeey review the full incident log for each studio

Incidents are categorised and trends are identified

Recurring issues trigger process or facility improvements

Incident data feeds into quarterly performance reviews

Resolution Closure

Every incident must have a 'closed' status update on the email thread

Closure update must include: what was done, what the client's final status is, and whether any further action is pending

Incidents may not be considered 'closed' until the client-facing resolution is confirmed
